Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your iPhone. CTRBOX does not use cloud sync and does not upload your financial information anywhere.

How do I export my data?

Open the Settings tab in CTRBOX and tap "Export". You can export to JSON for a full backup or CSV for spreadsheet use.

Can I import data from another finance app?

Yes — use the Import option in Settings to load transactions from a JSON or CSV file. Make sure your file follows the standard template.

How do I enable the passcode?

Go to Settings → Passcode and follow the prompts to set a 4-digit passcode. Your passcode is hashed and stored only on your device.

I forgot my passcode. What now?

For your security, CTRBOX cannot recover a forgotten passcode. You will need to uninstall and reinstall the app, which will erase all locally stored data. Please export a backup before doing this.

How do I track a new investment?

Open the Portfolio tab, tap the "+" button, and enter the symbol, asset class, quantity, and cost basis. CTRBOX will track it from there.

Can I capture a receipt from my photo library?

Yes. When adding a receipt, choose "Choose from Library" to import any image. CTRBOX supports pinch-to-zoom, pan, and rotation when viewing receipts.

Does CTRBOX require an account?

No. CTRBOX is account-free by default. Just install the app and start using it.

What currencies are supported?

CTRBOX supports all major world currencies. Set your default currency in Settings; you can record transactions in any supported currency.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling CTRBOX removes all locally stored data from your device immediately and permanently.

Response Time
