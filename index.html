Your money, your terms.

Track investments that matter.

Log stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, bonds, REITs, and commodities with detailed cost basis, current valuations, dividend yield, and unrealized gains. The dashboard surfaces your net worth at a glance, while the allocation chart reveals how your portfolio is actually positioned. Filter by asset class, search by symbol, and drill into any holding to see performance metrics that help you make smarter decisions.

Snap receipts the moment you spend.

Capture receipts with your camera or import from your photo library. Add merchant, totals, tax, tip, line items, tags, and payment method. Receipt images support pinch-to-zoom, pan, and rotation — every detail stays accessible.

Budget with intention.

Set monthly limits by category, watch progress bars fill in real time, and get instant warnings when spending approaches your ceiling. Categories like Food, Transport, and Entertainment come pre-configured and fully customizable.

Reach every goal.

Create savings targets for vacations, emergency funds, or that next big purchase. Add contributions incrementally, watch the circular progress ring fill, and never lose sight of what you're working toward.

Understand your money.

Six-month cash flow trends, top spending categories, savings rate, and a savings rate visualization turn raw transactions into real insight.

Stay private by design.

No accounts. No cloud sync. No tracking. All data lives on your device, protected by an optional four-digit passcode. Export to JSON or CSV whenever you need it.
