Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CTRBOX ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our iOS application. CTRBOX is designed from the ground up to keep your financial data on your device and out of our hands.

1. Information We Collect

Short answer: nothing leaves your device.

CTRBOX does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on remote servers. All financial information, transaction records, receipt photos, portfolio holdings, budgets, and goals you create are stored locally on your iPhone using the device's secure storage.

2. Information Stored on Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Transactions (amount, category, account, date, notes)
  • Receipt images and metadata (merchant, line items, totals, tax, tip)
  • Portfolio holdings (symbol, quantity, cost basis, asset class)
  • Budgets (category, monthly limit)
  • Savings goals (name, target amount, contributions)
  • Accounts (name, type, balance)
  • Optional 4-digit passcode (stored as a device-only hash)
  • User preferences (currency, theme settings)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself via the App's built-in JSON or CSV export feature.

3. Camera and Photo Library Access

CTRBOX requests access to your camera and photo library solely so you can capture or import receipt images. We do not upload, share, or transmit any captured image. Images remain stored locally on your device and are removed when you delete them in the App or uninstall CTRBOX.

4. Network Access

CTRBOX does not require an internet connection for core functionality. If you enable the in-app currency rate feature, CTRBOX may fetch publicly available exchange rate information from a third-party API. No personal or financial information is sent in these requests — only a request for currency reference data.

5. Analytics and Tracking

CTRBOX does not include any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools. We do not use cookies, beacons, fingerprinting, or third-party trackers of any kind.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your data with third parties — because we never have access to your data in the first place. Your financial information remains on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

CTRBOX is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because all data is stored locally and never transmitted, no such information could be collected by us.

8. Data Security

Data is protected by iOS's standard on-device storage mechanisms. You may optionally enable a 4-digit passcode to add an additional layer of access protection. For maximum security, we recommend keeping your iPhone locked with a strong device passcode and enabling full-device encryption (which is the default on iOS).

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data. At any time you can:

  • Export all your data as JSON or CSV from the Settings screen.
  • Delete individual transactions, receipts, holdings, budgets, or goals.
  • Enable or disable the optional passcode.
  • Uninstall CTRBOX to permanently remove all locally stored data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Because CTRBOX does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes — please review this page periodically.
